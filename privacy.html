Draft privacy policy

Privacy policy for this directory

OutdoorResortsGatlinburg.com is an independent directory for Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg at 4229 East Parkway, Gatlinburg, TN 37738. This draft explains what the directory collects when someone uses a form, what happens when a guest leaves for a booking site, and how cookies are handled today.

Who this draft covers

This site helps people find individually owned RV rentals and rentable RV lots, then book with the owner on that owner's page. It is not the resort office booking desk. Tent camping is not offered. The directory does not publish a phone number or an email address.

Forms on this site

Two Netlify lead forms are on the site. Each one includes a hidden honeypot field that people should leave blank. Netlify uses that field to filter spam.

The investor form on the lots-for-sale guide asks for a first name, last name, email address, an optional phone number that the person types in, which buying scenario fits, and a message. The directory uses that information to answer the question. The phone field is optional. Whatever someone types is not displayed on the site.

The List Your RV form asks for a name, email address, phone number, lot number, and iCal link. The directory uses that information to check that the listing is inside Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg and, if it is accepted, to publish the rental card the owner asked for. The phone number in that form is the number the owner types. It is not published as the directory's phone number.

Submitting a form does not reserve a stay and does not create a representation agreement. Netlify Forms stores the submission for the site operator. The directory does not sell form information. This draft does not set a retention period. That period needs the owner's decision.

Booking links

Rental cards link to the owner's page on Airbnb, VRBO, Hipcamp, or an owner-direct site. Choosing one of those links leaves this directory. The booking site has its own privacy policy, cookies, payment terms, and cancellation rules. This directory does not receive the booking payment and is not a party to the reservation. A link to Facebook or Google Maps also leaves this site.

Cookies and analytics

The pages include a Google Analytics 4 snippet that is commented out. It uses the placeholder measurement ID G-XXXXXXX, and it does not run. This directory does not set analytics cookies today.

If that snippet is turned on later, Google may set cookies such as _ga and receive pages you view, roughly where you are, and information about your browser. The owner should decide then whether a cookie notice is needed. Pages also load fonts from Google, so a browser request goes to Google. Netlify, which hosts the site, may keep ordinary server logs such as the address requested, the time, and an IP address.

Tennessee

The resort this directory describes is in Gatlinburg, Sevier County, Tennessee. This draft is written to be read under Tennessee law. The Tennessee Consumer Protection Act generally prohibits unfair or deceptive acts in trade. This directory does not sell the stay. Payment and cancellation terms belong to the owner and the booking site.

Tennessee also has the Tennessee Information Protection Act. That law applies only to controllers that pass specific size thresholds. This small directory does not believe it meets those thresholds. That is not a legal opinion, and the owner should have it checked before treating this draft as final.

Children

The directory is not directed to children under 13, and the forms are not meant to collect information from them.

Asking about a submission

Because the site does not publish an email address or a phone number, this draft does not name a separate privacy inbox. A person who wants a copy of a form submission, or wants it deleted, can say that in a later message through the same form. How those requests are handled still needs the owner's review.

The matching draft of the terms is at terms.html. Neither page is linked from the site footer or listed in the sitemap until the owner accepts the drafts.
