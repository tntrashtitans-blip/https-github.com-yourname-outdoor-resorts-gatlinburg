About the directory

About this Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg directory

OutdoorResortsGatlinburg.com is an independent directory for Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg at 4229 East Parkway, Gatlinburg, TN 37738. Compare RV rentals and rentable RV lots here, then book with the owner on the listing's own page. Tent camping is not offered.

Browse Rentals Read the Stay Guide

A directory, not the booking desk

This site is not the resort office booking desk. Listings are individually owned. Each rental card links to the owner's Airbnb, VRBO, Hipcamp, or owner-direct page. Payment, house rules, and cancellation terms belong on that booking page, not here.

The resort sits on East Parkway, about 10 minutes from downtown Gatlinburg, on the Greenbrier side of Great Smoky Mountains National Park. The guides on this site describe RV rentals, rentable RV lots, and resort amenities that include pools, fishing, putt-putt, playgrounds, a game room, and laundry. Practical stay questions belong in the stay guide.

Who runs it

Laramy Gregory, an owner at Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg, runs this directory. It is not the resort homeowners association and it is not resort management.

The investing and owner guides are written in the first person by an owner at the resort who describes himself as a licensed Tennessee real estate agent. Those pages carry that description. This page does not add a license number or a brokerage name.

Directory FAQ

Questions before you book

Is OutdoorResortsGatlinburg.com the resort office?

No. It is an independent directory, not the resort office booking desk. Guests book with the owner on Airbnb, VRBO, Hipcamp, or an owner-direct page.

Can I tent camp at Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg?

No. Tent camping is not offered. The directory lists RV rentals and rentable RV lots for guests who bring their own camper.
