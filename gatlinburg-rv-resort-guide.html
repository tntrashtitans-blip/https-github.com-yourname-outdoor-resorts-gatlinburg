RV resorts, RV parks, and campgrounds near Gatlinburg

A plain-language guide to RV-friendly stays on the east side of Gatlinburg, written from Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg at 4229 East Parkway. The official resort site is orgtn.net. Owners of rentals here run this directory so you can compare listings and book with the owner.

Start with the kind of stay

What an RV-friendly stay near Gatlinburg actually looks like

Searches for Gatlinburg campgrounds, Gatlinburg RV parks, and RV resorts in Gatlinburg, Tennessee land in one list. The stays behind those words are different. A campground near Gatlinburg might be a national park campground, a simple hookup park, or a private resort, and it might take tents, RVs, or both. Park campgrounds use the park's own reservation system. If that is the trip, start with the Great Smoky Mountains guide and the National Park Service. Come back here for a private RV resort outside the park.

An RV park, the way most guests mean it, is a place to put an RV for the night: a pad, hookups, and a way in and out. An RV resort is that, plus shared recreation so the day does not have to be a drive. Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg is the second kind. It is an RV resort on East Parkway, about 10 minutes from downtown Gatlinburg, on the Greenbrier side of the mountains. Tent camping is not offered.

That last sentence matters for the campground search. If the plan is a tent pad and no RV, this resort will not fit. The association's restated master deed, posted on orgtn.net, says ground tents are outside the camping units allowed on a site. Use a park campground for a tent, and use this resort when the night is an RV night.

The resort itself

What Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg offers

The association describes Outdoor Resorts at Gatlinburg as one of the older RV resorts in the area. It began in 1970. It has 376 sites. The people who own those sites make up the Association of Outdoor Resorts at Gatlinburg. The address on the official association site is the same address this directory uses: 4229 East Parkway, Gatlinburg, TN 37738.

Every site, the association says, has full hookups: 30-amp and 50-amp electric, water, sewer, cable TV, and Wi-Fi. Some sites can take trailers up to 42 feet. That is a resort-wide statement, not a promise about the one lot on a listing card. Match the lot number on the card to the map, then ask that owner whether your camper length, slides, and tow vehicle fit.

The on-site list from orgtn.net is specific. Two pools, an adult pool and a family pool. A stocked lake and two mountain streams, with Webb Creek named among the amenities. Basketball, miniature golf, shuffleboard, a children's playground, a family game room, picnic areas, and a coin-operated laundry. The stay guide adds the calendar note guests need: the resort pool is generally open from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend, and the host can confirm the week you are booking.

Common-area notes on the association site also tell guests how to share the place. Roads, the laundry, the rec area, and the lake paths are for getting around. Other people's lots are private. Campfires stay in the fire pit, and the association may restrict burning when conditions call for it. Those are resort rules, separate from the house rules on an Airbnb or VRBO listing.

Two ways to stay

How renting an owner's RV or lot works

Nothing on this website is a reservation. Owners of rentals at Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg run the directory. You compare listings here, then you book on the owner's own page. Payment, cancellation, pets, and the check-in note live there. The about page says the same thing in shorter form: this is a directory, and it is not the homeowners association and not resort management.

A Gatlinburg RV rental

On the rentals page, an RV rental or park model is the furnished unit. You arrive to a camper that is already on the lot. Sleep count, stairs, linens, and parking are the details that change the trip. Most lots hold one vehicle. Some hold two. The host's note is the one that counts.

A lot for your own camper

A lot rental is the other card on the same page. Book it only when the camper is coming with you. The association's hookup description is why people look here for a bring-your-own stay: full hookups, including 30- and 50-amp, on the sites it describes. You still confirm length, slide clearance, and which side of the resort the lot sits on. The map on the rentals page marks creekside, lake-area, hillside, and interior sections by lot number.

The association also tells RV owners they can reserve a site and bring their own RV, and it says owners rent furnished RVs on third-party booking sites. Use orgtn.net for the association's own path. Use this directory when you want the owner listings that have been tied to an ORG lot.

Rules that change the dates

The restated master deed on orgtn.net limits how a non-owner uses a site. As written there, a guest who is not an owner or an immediate family member has a maximum stay of three weeks, and at least fourteen days have to pass before that guest occupies the same site or another site. Read the current PDF on orgtn.net, and ask the host to confirm the rule against your dates before you plan a long stay. Deeds get amended.

Pets are a listing question plus a resort question. The stay guide says the resort is generally dog friendly, that each listing sets its own pet rule, and that visitors pay a pet permit of $10 per pet for the trip. Walk dogs on pathways or on the lot you rented.

Where the days go

Using the resort as a base, not as the whole trip

East Parkway is the useful part of the location. Downtown Gatlinburg is a short drive west, close enough for dinner and far enough that the night is quieter than a room on the Parkway. The dining guide and the things-to-do page both start from this address.

Dollywood is a Pigeon Forge day, not a walk from the lot. The Dollywood page covers the drive from East Parkway, where an RV can and cannot park, and how the 2026 festival calendar fits a resort night. Rocky Top Sports World is the other east-side trip, for tournament weekends when a kitchen and laundry matter.

The resort's own day is the one people skip when they only read attraction lists. Pools in season, the fishing lake, mini-golf, the game room, and the laundry are why a multi-night RV stay feels different from changing hotel rooms. A park day, a downtown evening, and a morning on the lot is a fuller Gatlinburg RV trip than three attraction days in a row.

Questions

Gatlinburg RV resort questions

Is Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg a campground near Gatlinburg?

It is an RV resort at 4229 East Parkway, Gatlinburg, TN 37738, for RV camping. Tent camping is not offered. The official association site is orgtn.net. This directory is independent of that site.

What is the difference between an RV resort, an RV park, and a campground?

A campground near Gatlinburg may allow tents, RVs, or both. An RV park is mainly a place to park an RV. An RV resort adds shared recreation. Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg is an owner-association RV resort.

How do Gatlinburg RV rentals work on this site?

Use the rentals page, then book with the owner on Airbnb, VRBO, Hipcamp, or an owner-direct page. A rental includes the RV. A lot is for guests who bring a camper. This directory does not take the reservation.

What does Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg offer?

The association site says the resort began in 1970, with 376 owned sites and full hookups: 30- and 50-amp electric, water, sewer, cable TV, and Wi-Fi. It lists two pools, a stocked lake, two mountain streams, basketball, mini-golf, shuffleboard, a playground, a game room, picnic areas, and coin laundry. Some sites take trailers up to 42 feet. Confirm the lot with that owner.

Can I reserve a site through this website?

No. Book the owner listing, or use the association's own bring-your-RV path on orgtn.net. Questions about a booked stay go to that owner.

Are there stay limits for renters?

The restated master deed on orgtn.net excludes ground tents and describes a three-week maximum for a non-owner occupant, then at least fourteen days away before another stay. Read the current deed and ask your host. Association documents can be amended.
