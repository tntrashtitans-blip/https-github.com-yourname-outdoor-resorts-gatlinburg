Christmas and winter in the Smokies from an RV

Home › Things to Do › Christmas and winter

Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg · Updated November 23, 2026

A Gatlinburg RV rental at Christmas is a softer mountain trip than July. The lights are on, Dollywood is dressed for the holidays, and the same afternoon can be mild in town and icy up high. Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg, at 4229 East Parkway, hosts many families who come to the Smokies for that week. The resort sits about 10 minutes from downtown.

This guide is for winter camping in Gatlinburg from a camper that is already here. It covers which rentals stay open, why a parked camper is the easier cold-weather plan, and how to line up the lights, Dollywood, and a park morning. OutdoorResortsGatlinburg.com is an owner-run rental directory. It is not the resort office, and it is not the HOA site at orgtn.net. You book on the listing. Tent camping is not offered.

Book Christmas and New Year's while the campers are still open

Most rental campers in the community are open through January 2. After that, many owners winterize their units until March 1. Christmas and New Year's sit in the open window. A dark calendar after January 2 usually means that camper is closed for the cold, not that the whole resort has shut.

Browse the Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg rentals and open each calendar. One camper can be free on December 23 while the next was booked in the fall. Confirm the dates on the booking page. The stay guide explains a furnished RV rental versus a lot if you bring your own camper.

A set-up rental beats towing in winter

A Gatlinburg Christmas RV that is already parked lets you skip the hard part of winter towing. The camper is leveled and hooked up on East Parkway. You drive the family car to Dollywood, the parade, and the lights, then come back to heat the host has already set up. Towing adds mountain grades, a chance of ice after dark, and the work of keeping your own water safe in a freeze.

If you bring your own rig, check lot length, hookups, and slide-out room, and ask the host how that lot is handled in a freeze. Heated hoses, skirting, and the furnace are listing details, not a promise from this directory.

Gatlinburg Winter Magic

The Gatlinburg Convention and Visitors Bureau describes Winter Magic as more than 30 years of millions of lights on displays through the city. You can see them from the car or on foot. The Visit Gatlinburg app has a self-guided lights tour and a scavenger hunt for 43 snowpeople downtown. A printable map is on that page.

The Pigeon Forge tourism site lists Gatlinburg Winter Magic from November 5, 2026, through February 15, 2027. The Gatlinburg page does not publish a nightly switch-on time, so check it again the week you travel. That events list also puts the Fantasy of Lights Christmas Parade on Friday, December 4, 2026, from 7:30 to 9:00 p.m. downtown, and the New Year's Eve ball drop and fireworks on December 31, 2026, from 9:30 p.m. to 12:30 a.m. at the Gatlinburg Space Needle. Confirm both. East Parkway keeps the late drive short.

Dollywood's Smoky Mountain Christmas

Dollywood lists Smoky Mountain Christmas presented by Humana from November 6, 2026, through January 3, 2027, with more than 6 million lights. The park's November 2, 2026, announcement describes evening snow in Wildwood Grove and Santa's last day on December 23. Dollywood's Smoky Mountain Christmas guide also lists the park closed on November 26, 2026, and on December 24 and 25, and it says the two water rides are closed for the season. Confirm your day there.

The Dollywood page walks through a back route from East Parkway toward Craftsman's Valley, which can keep a holiday morning out of the thickest Parkway traffic. Give the park its own day.

Pigeon Forge Winterfest

Pigeon Forge lists Winterfest lights from November 6, 2026, through January 31, 2027, nightly from 5 p.m. to 1 a.m. The free kickoff is Friday, November 13, 2026, from 4:00 to 8:00 p.m. at Patriot Park, with entertainment, ice skating, live reindeer, kids' activities, and fireworks. The page says millions of lights and gives more than one exact count, so treat the number loosely. Give Winterfest its own evening.

The national park in winter

Great Smoky Mountains National Park is open 24 hours a day, all year, and visitor centers are open year-round except Christmas Day. The seasonal road schedule says primary roads such as Newfound Gap Road (US 441) are open year-round, weather permitting. Kuwohi Road, formerly Clingmans Dome Road, is listed April 1 through November 29. Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail is listed April 17 through November 29. For Christmas, plan on those seasonal roads being closed.

Weather permitting still changes the day. On January 23, 2026, the park closed Newfound Gap Road between Gatlinburg and Cherokee for a winter storm, along with Greenbrier Road, the Cosby entrance, Little River Road, and other roads, until staff could check them. A clear sky in town does not mean the high road is dry. Look at current conditions the morning you go.

On the park's weather page, low-elevation winter days often reach 50 or warmer, while most nights fall to freezing or below. Temperatures can run 10 to 20 degrees colder up high. Snow of an inch or more is occasional in town and more common in the mountains, where a storm can leave as much as two feet. January and February are the most likely months. Gatlinburg's January averages on that page are a high of 51 and a low of 28.

From East Parkway, start with Greenbrier when that road is open. The Great Smoky Mountains page is built that way. If the road is closed, use the things to do page, including the Gatlinburg Community Center, instead of forcing a drive the park has already shut.

What to pack for a cold camper

Pack layers, a rain shell, a hat, gloves, and shoes with tread. The park tells hikers to carry rain gear and clothes that still warm you when wet. Bring warm sleep clothes and slippers, because RV floors get cold. Read the listing for linens and heat, then ask the host what is actually in the camper. Leave the pool bag unless the host says otherwise. The stay guide says the pool is generally open from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend. Keep grills, camp stoves, generators, and fuel heaters outside the RV and out from under a closed awning. If the camper is cold, ask how that unit's heat is meant to run.

A simple Christmas week from East Parkway

Use the arrival afternoon to learn the heat. Give Dollywood its own day, Winter Magic its own evening, and the park a morning with a town backup if a road closes. December 4 is the parade. December 31 is the ball drop. Leave one day mostly empty. Families come back for that slower rhythm, with a warm camper at the end of it.

Questions guests ask before they book

Are Gatlinburg RV rentals open for Christmas?

Most rental campers at Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg are open through January 2. After that, many owners winterize until March 1. Christmas and New Year's are in the open window and book early, so check each listing's calendar.

Can we tent camp at Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg for the holidays?

No. Tent camping is not offered. Book a furnished RV rental, or a lot if you are bringing your own camper.

When is Dollywood's Smoky Mountain Christmas?

Dollywood lists Smoky Mountain Christmas from November 6, 2026, through January 3, 2027. Its Christmas guide also lists closures on November 26 and on December 24 and 25. Santa's last day at the park is December 23. Confirm your date on that guide.

Does Newfound Gap Road stay open in winter?

The National Park Service says Newfound Gap Road is open year-round, weather permitting. Snow and ice can still close it. On January 23, 2026, the park closed it between Gatlinburg and Cherokee, along with Greenbrier Road and other roads. Check current conditions before you drive.

Is a set-up RV rental easier than towing in winter?

For most Christmas trips, yes. A rental camper is already parked at 4229 East Parkway, so you drive a regular car to the lights and Dollywood. Towing adds mountain grades, possible ice, and the work of keeping your own water safe in a freeze.

What should we pack for winter camping in Gatlinburg?

Pack layers, a rain shell, a hat, gloves, shoes with tread, and warm clothes for sleeping. Ask the host what linens and heat are included. The pool is generally a summer amenity, Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend.
