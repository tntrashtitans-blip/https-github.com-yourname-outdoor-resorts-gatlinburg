Draft terms

Terms for using this directory

These draft terms cover OutdoorResortsGatlinburg.com, an independent directory for RV rentals and rentable RV lots at Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg, 4229 East Parkway, Gatlinburg, TN 37738. People use it to find an owner's listing and book with that owner directly.

What this site is

The directory is not the resort office booking desk. It is not the resort homeowners association and it is not resort management. Listings are individually owned. Tent camping is not offered.

A rental card links to the owner's Airbnb, VRBO, Hipcamp, or owner-direct page. The booking, payment, house rules, and cancellation terms are between the guest and that owner on that other site. This directory does not take the reservation and does not promise that a date shown here is still open.

Forms

The investor form and the List Your RV form send a message to the directory operator through Netlify. Sending a form does not reserve a lot, does not list a property by itself, and does not create a representation agreement unless the operator later agrees to that in writing. The draft privacy policy describes what those forms collect.

Investor pages

Investor guides are informational. They are not legal, tax, lending, or financial advice. Examples of price, rent, fees, or financing are not a promise of results. Decisions about buying a lot should be reviewed with a licensed attorney, CPA, lender, and real estate professional.

Using the site

Do not use the directory to break the law, interfere with the site, scrape it in a way that degrades it, or submit a listing you do not have permission to share. Listing details, photos, prices, and availability can change on the booking site after they appear here.

The directory's text and page design belong to the site operator. Listing photos and descriptions may belong to the owner or the booking platform. Do not copy them for your own commercial use without permission.

Limits of this draft

The directory is offered as a planning aid. To the extent Tennessee law allows, the operator is not liable for a booking decision made on another website, for a listing that changed, or for a loss that comes from using the information pages. Some limits may not be enforceable, and this sentence is part of the draft the owner still needs to review.

Tennessee law

This draft is meant to be governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. A dispute about the directory would be heard in the state or federal courts located in Tennessee. Whether to name Sevier County in particular is still an owner decision, because that is the county where the resort sits.

The owner may replace this draft. The version on the site is the one that applies when you use the directory, once the owner has accepted it. Until then, treat this page as a draft. It is not linked from the footer or listed in the sitemap.
