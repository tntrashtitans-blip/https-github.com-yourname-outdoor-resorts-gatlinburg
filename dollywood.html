Dollywood Trip Planning

Dollywood lodging near Gatlinburg, with a smarter back route

Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg gives families a quieter East Parkway RV camping base for Dollywood days, with a practical route toward the park that can avoid the worst Parkway traffic getting in and out of Pigeon Forge.

Check Dollywood Calendar Buy Tickets

Why Stay Here

A calmer base for a Dollywood day

Dollywood is in Pigeon Forge, at 2700 Dollywood Parks Blvd. Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg is an RV resort at 4229 East Parkway, on the Gatlinburg side of the trip. The night is an RV night: a furnished rental or your own camper on an owner lot, with room to cook and reset after the park. Tent camping is not offered.

Ready-to-stay rentals Choose an RV or park model that is already set up inside the resort.
Bring your camper Book a rentable RV lot when your family prefers its own rig, then leave it on the lot for the park day.
A resort after the park Pools in season, the lake, mini-golf, and laundry are back at East Parkway, not in the Dollywood parking lot.

Distance and drive

The drive from 4229 East Parkway to Dollywood

Dollywood's directions say the park is less than 10 miles from the heart of Gatlinburg: US-441 north, then Dollywood Lane / Veterans Boulevard. That start is downtown. The resort is east of downtown on East Parkway, about 10 minutes from town, so add that leg. A mapped drive from 4229 East Parkway comes out around 19 to 22 miles and about 45 minutes with clear roads. A summer Saturday, a festival morning, or a Christmas evening can make the Parkway version much longer. Check a live map before you leave.

The Parkway route

Go west on East Parkway through Gatlinburg, then north on US-441, the Spur, and the Pigeon Forge Parkway. Dollywood Lane and Veterans Boulevard (TN-449) lead toward McCarter Hollow Road. This is the route Dollywood prints for Gatlinburg drivers, and it is the one the whole town is using when the strip is full.

The east-side route

The other mapped option stays east of downtown: East Parkway to Webb Creek Road, Pittman Center Road (TN-416), Jayell Road, and McCarter Hollow Road. Dollywood's Sevierville directions also finish on McCarter Hollow, so you approach from the Veterans Boulevard side. Clear-road time is similar. Take this route when a live map shows the Parkway stopped and these roads moving, including on the way home after closing. Roads close and get repaved. Dollywood's directions cover the last turn. The live map covers the traffic.

Parking

Where an RV guest actually parks

You sleep at the resort. Dollywood parking is daytime only. The parking page says overnight parking is not available at any Dollywood park, and RV parking is not for overnight parking or camping.

Leave the RV on the lot

Most furnished rentals should do this. Drive a car. Dollywood's parking page currently lists general parking at $25 a vehicle, with trams from lots A through E, and preferred parking at $55 for the day you select. Gold and Diamond passes include general parking under the rules printed there, and that page says the first parking fee for those passholders is non-refundable. Confirm the number on the parking page the week you go.

Drive the RV to the park

If the RV is your only vehicle, the same page lists oversized and RV parking at $30 in Lot F, a short walk to the front gate. Come back to the resort at closing. Same-day re-entry depends on space and a receipt. Pets are a separate plan: Dollywood allows animals only as ADA service animals, with Doggywood noted for dogs that have updated vaccinations. Resort pet rules are in the stay guide and on your listing.

2026 park calendar

Plan around Dollywood's season, not just the drive

Dollywood's 41st season opened March 13, 2026. The old theme-park calendar address no longer works. Use Dollywood's calendar before you book resort nights, and the festival page for what is playing. Dollywood published these 2026 windows:

  • I Will Always Love You Music Festival: March 13 through April 12.
  • Flower & Food Festival: April 18 through June 7.
  • Smoky Mountain Summer Celebration presented by Food City: June 15 through August 2, with Sweet Summer Nights.
  • Harvest Festival presented by Humana: September 14 through October 31, with Great Pumpkin LumiNights.
  • Smoky Mountain Christmas presented by Humana: November 6, 2026 through January 3, 2027, with six million lights. Dollywood says the park is closed November 26 and December 24 and 25.

For that Christmas run, Dollywood published hours of 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. on most nights, with 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. on November 27 and 28 and again December 26 through January 3. Hours can change. The lights mean many cars leave after dark, so the drive back to East Parkway is a different problem than a summer afternoon. Splash Country is a separate water park. Confirm its date on Dollywood's site if you want a water day in the same trip.

RV guests

Tips for a park day when you are staying in an RV

Spend a night at the resort before the park day. The RV resort guide explains the two bookings: a furnished RV, or a lot only if the camper is yours.

  • Buy tickets and parking from Dollywood. Pick-a-day tickets lock a date. Any-day tickets cost more when weather might move the day. Compare a pass if you expect more than one visit.
  • On Saturdays, holidays, and Christmas evenings, leave earlier than the clear-road time and have the east-side route ready in the map.
  • Eat one meal at the resort if the rental has a kitchen. For a table out, start with the East Parkway dining guide.
  • Pack layers. NightFlight Expedition is indoors, and most of the park is not. In summer, the stay guide says the resort pool is generally open Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day. Confirm that week with your host.
  • Save one day for the mountains or for the lot. The Great Smoky Mountains page covers the Greenbrier side of this address, and the things-to-do page covers the rest.

2026 headline ride

NightFlight Expedition, and why it matters on a wet day

Dollywood describes NightFlight Expedition as its largest attraction investment, more than $50 million, and as the world's first indoor family hybrid coaster and whitewater river raft ride. It opened for the 2026 season in Wildwood Grove.

The ride page puts it in a 44,000-square-foot building and lists a 5 1/2-minute ride with a 39-inch minimum. On a wet or cold day it is the indoor anchor. Confirm height and whether it is running that morning.

Questions

Dollywood lodging questions

How far is Dollywood from Outdoor Resorts Gatlinburg?

A mapped drive from 4229 East Parkway to 2700 Dollywood Parks Blvd is around 19 to 22 miles, about 45 minutes before Parkway traffic. Dollywood's downtown figure is shorter because the resort sits east of town. Check a live map.

Can I park an RV overnight at Dollywood?

No. Dollywood says RV parking is not for overnight stays or camping, and overnight parking is not offered at the parks. Sleep at the resort.

Which route should I compare from East Parkway?

Compare US-441 through Gatlinburg with the east-side roads: Webb Creek Road, Pittman Center Road, Jayell Road, and McCarter Hollow Road. Miles are similar in clear traffic. Use the east-side route when the Parkway is backed up.

Is this a hotel alternative for a Dollywood trip?

It is an RV resort stay, either a furnished RV or a lot for your own camper. Book on the rentals page and confirm details in the stay guide. This page is not Dollywood and not the official resort site.

Where do I buy Dollywood tickets?

Buy tickets, passes, and parking from Dollywood. Check the Dollywood calendar before you lock resort nights. This directory does not sell tickets.

Is tent camping available at the resort?

No. Tent camping is not offered. The stay is an RV rental, a park model, or a rentable RV lot.
